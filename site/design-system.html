Design system

Every value on the site comes from a token in styles.css. This page shows each one in use.

Color

White paper, cool grey floor, near-black ink. Cobalt is the brand and the only chromatic accent. Amber is reserved for reclaimable space, as in the app.

Elevation

Shadows are neutral and layered: a one-pixel edge, then offset and blur that double with each layer, so light comes from above. Small for controls, medium for panels and dialogs, large for the app window. Shadows only read on the floor, so surfaces that lift sit on it. App windows also get a hairline edge and an inner top highlight, like AppKit draws.

Index row

A name and a sentence, set on a ruled line. Used where content is a list of parallel things, in place of a card grid.

Plate

A floor-colored panel with a window cropped into it. The crop shows depth and keeps one screenshot from filling the page.

Rules

  • Sentence case everywhere. Buttons say what happens: Download, Copy, View source.
  • Headings carry the point. Structure such as rules, rows and plates must mean something.
  • One motion on load: the hero window rising into place. Everything else answers a click or hover. Reduced motion turns it off.
  • No green. Cobalt for action, amber for space you can reclaim.
